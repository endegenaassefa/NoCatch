## What this does NOT prove

G0 is platform smoke only: a root process *can* do the capture/overlay/hotkey
work in this GUI session and *cannot* be killed by uid 501. It says nothing
about what LDB does when capture fires with no killable GUI target present —
that is **G3a**, still unobserved, and remains the go/no-go for the whole
design.
